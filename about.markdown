I worked many different jobs that led me to my coding career, from things such as sports coaching, locally and internationally, to working as an EMT. Though my story might sound different at a glance, each step was an important part of the journey that led me to Software Development. Currently, I can bring a strong sense of team to any environment, stepping up in a leadership role or effectively working under instruction. I am process oriented, taking the appropriate time to learn and understand a situation, then working with others to design a satisfying solution. And fortunately, I'm a quick learner, just learning his way through this new stage of life. 

Hobbies include TTRPGs, staying active and healthy (volleyball, basketball, lifting), and spending time with those who like spending time with me!
